Idea Forge

A two-round creative problem-solving workout. First, race the clock finding surprising uses for an everyday object. Then mash up two random objects into a brand-new invention. There are no wrong answers here — only more ideas.

Why this works: Fluency (how many ideas you can generate) and forced connections (combining unrelated things on purpose) are two classic techniques for building creative problem-solving muscle. Quantity breeds quality — the goal is to get past your first three obvious answers.
LearnPad · Idea Forge is a creativity warm-up game, not a formal aptitude assessment.
