North Star

You're leading a small crew on an expedition into new territory. Five scenarios, three choices each. Every decision shifts two things: how clearly you paint the horizon (Vision), and how much your crew trusts you to get them there (Trust). There's no single right answer — real leadership is a balancing act.

Why this works: Visionary leadership research consistently points to two things great leaders balance: a clear, compelling direction, and the trust of the people following them. Vision without trust feels lonely at the top. Trust without vision feels safe but stalled. This game lets you feel that tradeoff, choice by choice.
