Mind Games

Free browser games for two things worth practicing: feeling a little better, and getting a little sharper. Some lift your mood with gratitude and breathing. Others are quick, playful workouts for creativity, leadership, mental flexibility, resilience, and entrepreneurial thinking.

🌻

Sunny Meadow

Grow a garden by planting gratitude, popping heavy thoughts into balanced ones, breathing through a calming round, and picking a tiny win. Your garden — and your streak — grow with you over time.
