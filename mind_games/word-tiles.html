Why this works: Building the highest-scoring word from a random handful of letters is a classic combinatorial thinking exercise — you're weighing vocabulary against arithmetic, deciding whether a longer word or a well-placed high-value letter wins. Word Tiles uses a curated common-word list rather than a full professional dictionary, so a few obscure-but-valid words may not be recognized yet.
