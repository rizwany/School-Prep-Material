Why this works: Matchstick puzzles are a classic constraint-based reasoning exercise — you have to mentally simulate a change before committing to it, weighing several plausible moves against one fixed rule (only one stick moves). That kind of look-before-you-leap thinking is exactly what creative problem-solving looks like under a real constraint.
