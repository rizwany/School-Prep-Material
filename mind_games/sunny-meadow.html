Today's activities
Why these four? Gratitude noticing, reframing harsh thoughts, slow breathing, and small achievable actions are simple, well-known low-pressure ways people support their own mood day to day. Sunny Meadow just makes them a little more fun to repeat.
This is a game, not a treatment. Sunny Meadow can be a nice part of a day, but it isn't therapy and can't diagnose or treat depression. If low mood, low energy, or loss of interest lasts most of the day for two weeks or more — or you ever have thoughts of harming yourself — please reach out to a trusted person or a qualified professional now.
