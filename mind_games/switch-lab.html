Switch Lab

Sort each card onto one of the four key cards below. There's a rule — maybe it's color, maybe it's shape, maybe it's how many symbols are on the card. You won't be told which. Watch the ✅ / ❌ feedback, and when the rule quietly changes, adapt as fast as you can.

Why this works: This game is inspired by classic card-sorting tasks used to study cognitive flexibility — the mental skill of letting go of a pattern that no longer works and adopting a new one quickly. The faster you recover after a rule change, the more flexible your thinking in the moment.
LearnPad · Switch Lab is a cognitive flexibility warm-up, not a diagnostic test.
